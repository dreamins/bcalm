# bcalm privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

bcalm is an anxiety and trigger logger. This page says what the app does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your phone unless you choose it.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. The developer has no server and receives no data from the app.

## Your entries

Everything you log (anxiety ratings, causes, sleep, caffeine, medication notes, calm cards, support contacts, settings) is stored on your device only.

## Optional sync

If you turn on sync, the app writes a CSV file to a location you pick with Android's standard file picker, for example a file in your own Google Drive. The app reads and writes only that file. Where it goes after that is governed by the provider you chose (such as Google Drive), not by the developer. You can also export or import a CSV manually. Turning sync off stops writing to the file.

## Notifications

Check-in and follow-up notifications are scheduled and shown locally on your device. No notification service is used.

## Network use

The app has the INTERNET permission for one purpose: the "Check for updates" button in Settings. Only when you tap it, the app downloads a small public file (`version.json`) from `https://github.com/dreamins/bcalm/releases/download/latest/version.json`, which lists the latest version number and download link. No information about you or your entries is sent, other than what any web request normally reveals to GitHub (such as your IP address). The app makes no other network requests.

## Sharing and deletion

Your data is not shared with anyone by the developer. To delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, and delete any CSV file you created for sync.

## Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at https://github.com/dreamins/bcalm/issues
